# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple project that calculates the
simple interest and total amount based on the principal amount, rate of
interest, and time period.

## Formula

The formula used to calculate simple interest is:

**Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100**

The total amount is calculated as:

**Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest**

## Inputs

The calculator requires three inputs:

- **Principal (P):** The initial amount of money.
- **Rate (R):** The annual rate of interest in percentage.
- **Time (T):** The time period in years.

## Example

Suppose:

- Principal = ₹10,000
- Rate = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Then:
